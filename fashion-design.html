Fashion Design

Editorial Web Design

Project Overview

An editorial layout project focused on translating high-end print design principles into a digital format. Inspired by the July 2026 issue emphasizing "The Year of Elegance," this design balances striking photography with meticulous typographic hierarchy to showcase top trends for the spring and summer seasons.

Role

Editorial Designer

Tools

Photoshop, Figma, Illustrator

Timeline

3 Weeks

Design Philosophy

High-end fashion editorial requires a delicate balance between arresting imagery and readable typography. Taking inspiration from the article "Why Minimalism is the Future of Style," the layout strips away unnecessary visual noise. It relies on a soft pink and crisp white color palette to make the central subject stand out.

Classic serif typography is used for the masthead to establish authority and elegance, while modern, clean sans-serif fonts are applied to the cover lines to maintain a contemporary, minimalist aesthetic.

Technical Implementation

The image underwent extensive color grading to ensure skin tones remained natural while perfectly matching the stylized background. Masking techniques were utilized to layer the model seamlessly in front of the masthead text, creating a dynamic three-dimensional effect commonly seen in premium publications.

The grid system was rigorously mathematically aligned to ensure that elements like the barcode, date, and feature headlines rest in perfect optical balance without overcrowding the visual space.
