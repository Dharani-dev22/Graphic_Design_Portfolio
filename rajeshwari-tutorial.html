Rajeshwari Tutorial

Logo & Brand Identity

Project Overview

The objective was to create a distinguished and trustworthy brand identity for an educational institution. The design needed to convey academic excellence, stability, and growth while remaining highly legible across both print and digital mediums.

Role

Graphic Designer

Tools

Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop

Timeline

6 hours

Design Philosophy

The logo utilizes a classic crest or shield motif, which is universally recognized as a symbol of education and institutional integrity. I incorporated a bold, serif 'R' to establish immediate brand recognition, set against an open book to symbolize learning and knowledge.

The color palette is deliberately grounded. The deep forest green evokes growth, tradition, and focus, while the gold accents communicate premium quality and achievement. This combination ensures the brand feels established and professional.

Typography & Construction

The typography balances tradition with modern readability. A clean, sans-serif font was selected for the primary "Rajeshwari Tutorial" text to ensure it remains legible even when the logo is scaled down for business cards or social media avatars.

The concentric circles binding the elements together represent unity and a holistic approach to education. The subtle drop shadow applied to the central 'R' adds depth, allowing the primary lettermark to stand out against the geometric background.
