Jaya Balaji Super Market

E-commerce Mobile App • UI/UX Design

Project Overview

Jaya Balaji Super Market is a mobile shopping application designed to simplify grocery shopping through a clean, user-friendly interface. The project focuses on making everyday essentials easy to browse, search, and purchase while providing a smooth and intuitive shopping experience.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Project Type

Personal Concept

Tools

Figma, Photoshop

The Challenge

Traditional grocery shopping apps often display large amounts of information, making it difficult for users to quickly find products. The challenge was to design an interface that feels simple, organized, and accessible for users of all ages.

Design Solution

The interface was designed with a clear visual hierarchy, easy-to-recognize product categories, and a minimal checkout flow. Consistent spacing, clean typography, and intuitive navigation help users browse products effortlessly while maintaining a modern shopping experience.

Design System

The visual identity uses soft colors, rounded components, bold typography, and consistent spacing to create a welcoming shopping environment. Every screen follows a unified design language that strengthens usability and reinforces the supermarket's brand identity.
