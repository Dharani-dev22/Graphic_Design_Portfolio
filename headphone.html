Urbanista Miami

Product Design & Visualization

Project Overview

A technical visualization and layout design for the Urbanista Miami (Ruby Red) advanced high-fidelity wireless headphones. The objective was to create a clean, informative product showcase highlighting its ergonomic features, active noise cancellation capabilities, and premium build quality.

Role

Visual Designer

Tools

Figma, Photoshop

Timeline

3 hours

Ergonomics & Control

The design focuses heavily on user comfort, featuring a 15° pivot ergonomic arc and an adjustable memory band to ensure a perfect over-ear fit. The interface integrates intuitive on-ear controls for seamless playback, volume adjustment, and instant voice assistant integration without needing to reach for a device.

Audio immersion is achieved through Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation, utilizing an advanced transparency mode paired with a multi-microphone array specifically engineered for wind-noise reduction.

Technical Specifications

Physical Specs: The headset weighs a comfortable 280g, constructed from premium aluminum and memory foam for a luxurious over-ear fit.

Power & Connectivity: Equipped with Bluetooth 5.0, it delivers up to 50 hours of battery life with ANC off, and features a rapid 1.5-hour charge time via USB-C.

Audio Performance: Driven by 40mm dynamic drivers, it boasts an impedance of 32 Ohm and a wide frequency response of 20Hz - 20kHz for high-fidelity sound reproduction.
